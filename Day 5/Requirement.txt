1 -> Requirement analysis is the process of gathering, analyzing, documenting, validating, and managing requirement of software system

STEPS IN REQUIREMENT ANALYSIS 

Requirement elicitation 

Collect requirement from stakeholder, customers, and users. Methods include interviews, surveys

Requirement Analysis

Analyze the collected requirements and remove duplicates requirements and check whether document is feasible

Evaluation and Synthesis

Determine the necessary system functions. Understand data flow, system behavior

Requirement Specification

Document all requirements clearly in the Software Requirements Specification document

Requirement Validation

Verify the documented requirements with stakeholders to ensure that the requirements are complete, correct, and achievable.

Requirement Management

Track and manage requirement changes throughout the project lifecycle
