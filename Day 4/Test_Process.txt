1 -> Test Plan Activities

Defining Objectives -> Establish clear testing objectives aligning overall project goals

Scope Definition -> Determining scope of testing what will be tested and what will be exclude

Strategy And Approach -> Outline test strategy including type of testing

Resource Allocation -> Identifying the necessary resources, tools, environments and budget.

Schedule & Milestones -> Create a detailed testing schedule with defined milestones, dependencies, and timelines 

Risk Assessment and Mitigation -> Identify potential risks to the testing process 

Entry and Exit Criteria -> Define clear criteria for entering and exiting each test phase.

Test Deliverables -> Specify the expected outputs of the testing process, such as test plans, test cases, defect reports, summary reports.

Test Monitoring Activities

Tracking Progress -> Continuously track the progress of testing activities against the planned schedule

Metrics Collection -> Gather data such as defect discovery rate, defect density, and test coverage.

Status Reporting -> Periodically report on the status of testing to stakeholders

Performance Measurement -> Monitor the performance of the software under test, collecting data on response times

Risk Monitoring -> Continuously monitor identified risks and assess the emergence of new risks.
