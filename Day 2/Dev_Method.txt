1 -> Advantages And Disadvantage of Waterfall Model

Advantages of Waterfall Model

Simple and easy to understand – The process is straightforward and well-structured
Clear documentation – Each phase is documented thoroughly, making maintenance easier.
Easy to manage – Fixed stages and milestones make project tracking simple.
Well-defined requirements – Works effectively when project requirements are clear and unlikely to change.
Suitable for small projects – Ideal for projects with stable and well-understood requirements.

Disadvantages of Waterfall Model

Inflexible to changes – Making changes after a phase is completed is difficult and expensive.
Late testing – Testing occurs only after development is complete, so defects may be discovered late.
High risk for complex projects – If requirements are misunderstood, correcting them later can be costly.
Working software delivered late – The customer does not see a usable product until the end of the development cycle.
Not suitable for dynamic projects – Projects with frequently changing requirements are better suited to iterative or agile approaches.


2 ->  Kanban

Kanban is a visual framework aimed at managing work in progress and maximum efficiency

It works on physical or digital Kanban board it consists of To-Do, In Progress, Testing, Done

Teams restrict how many task can be in single column at once to prevent bottlenecks 

It is continuous and flow-based working process
