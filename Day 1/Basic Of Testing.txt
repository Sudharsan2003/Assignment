1) Principles Of Testing

    * Testing show presence of defects not thier absence -> Testing can reveal bug but cannot prove Software error free

    * Exhaustive Testing impossible - > Testing only focus on high-risk areas
    
    * Early testing Saves time and Money - > Finding defects in design phase is less expensive 

    * Defects cluster Together - > Some module has more defects

    * The pesticide paradox - > Repeating same test cases finding new defects

    * Testing is context-dependent - > Testing differs based on application type (eg-> ecommerce, banking, gaming)

    * The absence of errors fallacy - > Software with error free or few defects but it does not matched user requirement

2) Difference Between QA AND QC

OA - > Quality Assurance focus on preventing defects, it follows process oriented approach, it was proactive happen throughout the software development lifecycle and responsible for whole      team

QC - > Quality Control focus on finding defects, it follows product oriented approach, it was reactive happen after development or during testing and mainly done by testers
